# Editing Spec — Persian Reels Video Editor

## Timeline defaults

- Master format: 1080×1920, 9:16.
- Frame rate: preserve source FPS unless there is a clear reason to normalize.
- Do not crop out hands, products, phone screens, or other scenario-critical objects.
- Keep important overlays away from the top/bottom social UI zones and away from the face.

## Cut logic

Prefer semantic cuts:

1. Remove dead air before the hook.
2. Remove obvious false starts and duplicate wording when meaning is unchanged.
3. Keep micro-pauses that make speech sound human.
4. Add punch-ins on high-value words or reveal moments, not on a fixed interval.
5. Use B-roll/graphics only where they clarify or reinforce the spoken point.

## Subtitle rules

- Persian RTL only unless the source contains a necessary English brand/product term.
- Correct spacing and نیم‌فاصله.
- Break lines by phrase meaning.
- Prefer 1–2 lines per subtitle card.
- Avoid more than roughly 12–16 Persian words in one subtitle event unless pacing forces it.
- Keep strong contrast and a clean outline/shadow only when needed for readability.
- Do not place subtitles directly under the speaker's mouth.

## Motion rules

- Hook: fast but clean entrance, 150–300 ms feel.
- Emphasis: small scale/position motion, not large distracting sweeps.
- UI demonstrations: cursor/tap/click motion can be sharper.
- Proof/payoff: let the frame breathe slightly longer.
- CTA: keep readable and stable; avoid chaotic exit motion.

## Split composition

Use split-screen when the scenario contains a simultaneous explanation + visual example. Keep the speaker large enough to retain presence. Typical compositions:

- speaker top / demo bottom
- speaker left / demo right
- speaker in lower half / graphic in upper half

Choose based on where the subject already sits in the source frame. Never crop just to satisfy a template.

## Sound

- Dialogue is always priority one.
- Duck music and SFX under speech.
- Use one sound cue per meaningful visual event; do not attach sounds to every text animation.
- Avoid harsh notification sounds unless explicitly appropriate.

## Export quality gate

Before final delivery verify:

1. Final aspect ratio is 9:16.
2. No subtitles or overlays are clipped.
3. Persian text renders correctly.
4. Audio is synchronized.
5. No accidental silent gaps were introduced.
6. No asset blocks the speaker's face or key demo content.
7. Final MP4 plays from start to finish.
8. SRT timestamps match the rendered edit.
